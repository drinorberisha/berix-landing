BERIX / Engineering in Motion
Original case-study visualization studies, 12 November 2026

REVIEW
Local route: /motion-studies
Three named films share the BERIX ivory, olive, walnut, and cherry palette.
Their geometry, camera paths, and information behavior are deliberately distinct.
The storyboards are nine exact frames from the final authored scenes.

CONTRACT LOOM / PAYROLL INTEGRATION
Twenty illustrative provider lanes carry differently structured records through
thin optical schema boundaries. Fields normalize into a consistent output ribbon.
A duplicate joins the same contract without producing another output; a separate
retry route remains visible while the other records continue. Camera moves reveal
the contract, the moving fields, and the retry route before returning to the full
many-to-one system. This is contract normalization, not payment settlement.

CITATION FIELD / LEGAL RETRIEVAL
Twenty-one detailed illustrative source fragments form a staggered spatial index.
Open offset arcs represent retrieval; query tokens, source highlights, passages,
and citation pulses continue to move. Three extracted passages travel toward an
evidence surface while maintaining their source routes. Camera moves prioritize
the passages and their provenance. No actual client or legal text is present.
The scene illustrates retrieval and does not assert that an answer is legally
correct or that a nearest-neighbor result is verified advice.

SIGNAL SURFACE / EDGE ANOMALY DETECTION
Eight continuous signal filaments travel through 128 scoring cells. A distinct
cherry waveform appears in one ordinary local cell, rather than a central scoring
machine. Its independent alert route leads to the evidence surface while the
surrounding streams continue. A macro view establishes the local response, then
the camera widens to reveal distribution.

SOURCE AND REPRODUCTION
payroll.blend, legal.blend, and edge.blend are local editable Blender 5.2.0 LTS
masters, ignored by Git. Revision 5 is also saved in each respective Higgsfield
3D Jutsu project; the project links are recorded in manifest.json. Each has
semantic objects, Principled materials, a camera, a Sun/Point light rig, timeline
markers, and keyed motion. There are no external textures or imported models.

build_payroll.py, build_legal.py, and build_edge.py reproduce the complete final
scenes. Each begins with a guard requiring a NEW EMPTY scene. They intentionally
do not overwrite any existing file. Keep the original sources; save edits as a
separate copy. common.py and the three shorter builders retain the authoring
parts used to construct the full scripts.

Each source contains a 12-second loop sampled at 30 fps: frame 1 is the first
pose, and frame 361 is the duplicate loop endpoint. Source camera choreography
is stored in BERIX_camera_shots as five [position, target] pairs in Blender
coordinates. The browser converts [x,y,z] to [x,z,-y].

DELIVERY
Animated GLBs in ../../public/motion-studies preserve geometry and animation.
The browser uses a procedural studio environment for reflections, with its own
light balance. Its appearance is a web PBR interpretation of the source scene;
world lighting and all Blender features do not transfer identically through GLB.

The film previews are captured frame by frame from those animated exports at
960 x 600 and 24 fps. Playback timing is independent of the graphics capability
of the visitor's device. They are rendered authored motion, not AI video from a
single image. Optional interactive viewing retains the 3D source and camera
inspection. Reduced-motion visitors receive a poster until they choose Play.

The optional capture query parameter and bridge exist only to reproduce the
frame-by-frame web render; ordinary visitors see the review player.

PROVENANCE AND LIMITS
The case-study context and displayed figures come from the supplied public BERIX
Selected Work section at https://berixsystems.com/. Figures are labeled reported
project outcomes; the artwork is not a literal client topology or a measured
timing simulation. No client names, logos, real records, or confidential documents
were introduced. The source geometries are artistic representations, not exact
counts of all providers or indexed documents.

manifest.json records project links, exact revisions, file hashes, and source
loop checks. Source world transforms return to their opening pose within floating
point tolerance. Native lit macro frames and the nine-frame web storyboards were
visually inspected. Hardware-accelerated interactive frame rate was not verified
in the software-rendered test browser; the films are the default delivery.

Existing Connected Core work is preserved locally and ignored by Git. The website
publishes the films, posters, and animated GLBs from public/motion-studies through
the GitHub Pages workflow. The builders, storyboards, and manifest stay versioned;
local verification output and Blender master files stay outside Git.
